Penguin 3¶

Penguin 3 is a game coming after Penguin 2 and 1, where a penguin collects garbage and does their daily life.
The game uses the infrastructure of PengLang for the windows.

Garbage¶

Garbage is an important part of Penguin 3. Penguins can use it to sell for money.

Collecting garbage¶

The penguin can collect garbage in Penguin 3 using commands like collect-garbage.
It is ran in the main window, and does not open any other windows.

Waiting time of collecting garbage¶

Collecting garbage takes around 2 to 5 seconds. The waiting time of collecting garbage is not weighted.

Garbage collection amount¶

The amount of garbage collected every collection is 3 to 6 kilograms. The chances are not weighted.

Selling garbage¶

Selling garbage will open a window titled "sell garbage", where the player is presented with a label saying "sell your garbage" and how much garbage the player's penguin has in kilograms.

The player can sell garbage using the entry box at the bottom of said window where it says "Type a number, like 3 or type exit to go back..." as placeholder text. The player can follow the aforementioned text and press enter and sell garbage.

Garbage selling waiting time¶

There is no waiting time to sell garbage. It happens immediately.

Garbage selling earnings amount¶

The amount of money the player earns depends on randomness and the amount of garbage the player sells.

The earnings is the amount of garbage sold multiplied by a number between 0.5 and 2.

People call the numbers below 1 a bad market and above 1 a good market.

Garbage selling image¶

There is an image beside the entry box showcasing a humorous image of a penguin selling garbage at a stand saying "buy my garbage" at the top and then "please" at the bottom of the stand. Garbage are surrounded around it.

Main window¶

The main window of Penguin 3, titled after the game's name, Penguin 3, is a terminal-like window that is in a frame.
Here is a bullet-point-rendition of it:

  • Window
  • Frame
    • Textbox
    • Entry with the placeholder text: "Type here..."
    • Exit button
    • An image of the game's logo

Game logo¶

The logo of the game is an icon for Penguin 3. It appears everywhere in the game, and there are a few commands to trigger it opening it in a window, letting users see the logo in native resolution.

The logo starts off at the top, where a green penguin with a yellow beak is faced at the screen.
After that, a 3D number 3, the 3rd number of the positive integer set that is green, with a shade of dark green, around the corners of it. The logo also has a transparent background.

The logo was made in Microsoft Paint, a product made by Microsoft.

Government treasury¶

The government treasury in Penguin 3 is a file that has 2 comments, they say "Here lies government treasury. where is it"

This tells source code readers to stop reading the source code and think about their vibrant life for a second.

It also means there's no government treasury in the game.

Keybinds¶

There are no keybinds in Penguin 3, as it is meant to be typed, not memorized.

PengLang¶

PengLang is a Python module that makes Python for penguins. Penguin 3 uses PengLang for their simple windows, built off CustomTkinter.

Window workings¶

Using PenguinIceWindow, the main window can be made in a few lines.

Functions can be added to the keywords list as a tuple or a string, and they can be run in-game.

The arguments of a function get passed onto PengLang, and PengLang can make them become sub-keywords to pass to the function.

Sub-keywords can be made using the argument split, which is most commonly " " (a space).

Jimmy Jenguin¶

Jimmy Jenguin is not stated in Penguin 3, because it is part of an event that happened in PengLang's docs' Features section's Random Ice page. However, Jimmy Jenguin was a leader of the jenguins who fought against the penguins "due to many things". Jimmy is known for wanting the penguin's valued secrets, and they were surprised when the Bajida Karut™ was hidden behind a safe only penguin's knew about. They then said that they did all the work for nothing. After the penguins developed many innovative things, the jenguins had surrendered.

Sweesh swash¶

In Penguin 3, if the penguin turns right on a roundabout, they get sweesh swashed.

Store¶

The store of Penguin 3 is handled in a new window. It states a welcome, and how much money the penguin has. The statement updates every time the penguin buys something. There are many things to buy, including the Penguin Fries, Watermelon and the Pumpkin.

Pumpkin¶

The pumpkin is a feature of Halloween that introduces the decorating of Pumpkins. Pumpkins can be found on the tree, or be bought at the store for $15.23.
The player can decorate the pumpkin anywhere, including the beach and the spaceship.

The pumpkin will remember where its placed at, keeping a permanent memory of where it is.

Sidewalk finds¶

The penguin can find a banana on the sidewalk. This means other penguins commonly drop bananas every few hours, which means lucky finds are available.

Dirt¶

Dirt can be acquired by cleaning the ice floor. Penguins can use them to fill up potholes, but it may lead to consequences.

Pothole¶

Potholes in Penguin 3 can be filled up using dirt. The amount of dirt ranges from an integer around 10 to 20.

The penguin is forced to fill up the pothole immediately with no confirmation due to how ugly and disturbing it is, and if they don't have enough dirt, they will be forced to walk away and leave it behind, knowing the sins they did.

This is an important reason why holding dirt is a good idea.

Dirty cash¶

There is a small chance that if the amount of dirt collected is at least 60, that half of the penguin's cash gets dirty, and it cannot be used again.

This is an important reason why holding dirt is a bad idea.
